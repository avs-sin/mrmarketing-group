### Overview

Saffron Lounge is the standout lounge in Vegas Chinatown. It has the room, the drinks, and the crowd. It needed the strategy to match.

MR Marketing Group runs the full agency play: a multi-phase brand awareness campaign, ongoing retainer management, and liquor brand partnerships.

### The Challenge

Chinatown is one of the most competitive nightlife corridors in Las Vegas. Standing out takes more than a good night:

- Build brand awareness in phases, so each push lands before the next begins.
- Manage a retainer that covers strategy, content, and execution every month.
- Secure liquor brand partnerships that add value to the guest experience.
- Keep the lounge top of mind for locals and visitors alike.

The client needed one team that could own the whole brand, not a vendor for each piece.

### The Problem

A lounge with no strategy is just a room with a bar. Awareness fades between events. Partnerships stay one-off. Nobody owns the long game.

### The Solution

We built the campaign in phases, each with a clear goal and a clear message. Retainer management keeps social media consistent and on brand. Liquor brand partnerships bring in product, budget, and reach that the lounge could not get alone.

### Conclusion

Saffron Lounge shows what full agency strategy looks like in nightlife. Phased awareness, steady management, and partners who bring something to the table.
