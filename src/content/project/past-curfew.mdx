### Overview

Past Curfew: On the Hotline is an R&B party series and one of Las Vegas&apos;s most talked-about nightlife experiences.

MR Marketing Group handles the marketing: flyer design, social promotion, and event strategy. The music sets the mood. We make sure the city knows about it.

### The Challenge

A party series has to build momentum from one date to the next. Every flyer, every post, every event has to feed the next one:

- Design flyers that carry a recognizable look across the whole series.
- Promote each date on social without repeating the same post.
- Plan event strategy that grows the crowd instead of resetting it.
- Match the tone of an R&B night, not a generic club promo.

The series needed marketing with the same energy as the room.

### The Problem

Most party promotion is a flyer and a prayer. One post, one date, no plan for what comes after. The crowd shows up once and forgets.

### The Solution

We gave Past Curfew a consistent visual identity, a social promotion cadence for each date, and an event strategy that treats the series as one story. Every night points to the next one.

### Conclusion

Past Curfew: On the Hotline shows how flyer design, social promotion, and strategy turn a party into a series people talk about.
