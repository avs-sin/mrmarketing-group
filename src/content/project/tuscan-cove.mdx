### Overview

Two short vertical films from our selected work feature Tuscan Cove Bar + Patio in Las Vegas. Each one promotes a limited-time menu item, made on camera with the people behind it.

### What the films show

- **Antonio Loves Wings.** A wings special offered for a month at Tuscan Cove. The chef makes a chili chimichurri with honey, lemon, habanero, jalapeños, garlic, and cilantro, and the wings are plated and tasted on camera.
- **The Bullet Head burger.** A creator collaboration: a custom burger named after the creator, with pepper jack cheese, bacon, sautéed jalapeños, barbecue sauce, and the chef’s house sauce, offered for a month.

Both films close with an invitation to come to Tuscan Cove. Captions are available in the player.
