### Overview

Tuscan Cove Bar + Patio is a Las Vegas bar built for the crowd that wants a game on, a drink in hand, and a reason to stay.

MR Marketing Group handles the events that fill the patio: watch party promotions, executive dinners, and brand activations. That includes a March Madness campaign sponsored by Telson Tequila.

### The Challenge

A bar lives and dies by its calendar. Every week needs a reason to come in:

- Promote watch parties in a city with a sports bar on every corner.
- Position executive dinners as a premium experience, not a group booking.
- Bring liquor brand partners into activations that feel natural to the room.
- Design flyers that stop the scroll and match the vibe of the venue.

The client needed event marketing that moves fast and looks sharp.

### The Problem

Most bar promotions are an afterthought. A flyer goes up the day before. Nobody sees it. The patio stays half empty on a night that should be packed.

### The Solution

We plan the calendar ahead, design every flyer to the brand, and push each event on Instagram with a clear hook. For March Madness, we paired the watch party program with Telson Tequila as sponsor, so the brand, the bar, and the crowd all won the same night.

### Conclusion

Tuscan Cove proves that event marketing is the engine of a great bar. Give people a reason, make it look good, and put it where they will see it.
